      SafeSense is a decision aid, not a guarantee. A low score does not prove a message is safe. If you are unsure, contact the sender using details you already trust.

      How it works

      01

      Rules engine

      Checks links for look-alike spelling, fake brand names, hidden destinations and shorteners, and compares the sender name with the real address.

      02

      ML classifier

      A scikit-learn model (TF-IDF + logistic regression) learned the wording patterns of phishing, such as urgency, threats and requests for credentials.

      03

      Explained verdict

      Both scores are blended into one risk level, and every warning sign is listed in plain English with what to double-check.
